## Comprehension

Use the existing [two-round moderated protocol](experience-v8-usability-protocol.md):
six new representative visitors per round; at least five of six unassisted
completions per task in round two; zero unresolved critical misunderstandings.
Keep the five tasks and success criteria. For this candidate, construction
inspection is reached through the demo; the homepage no longer contains an
inspection interface. Record that discovery path without supplying the button
name or route as a hint.

Add two separate qualitative investor reviews after an unaided first impression:

- What does GridNinja offer today, and what is still being developed?
- What makes its technical approach distinctive, and what further evidence would
  you require before relying on its capacity or commercial claims?

Record their answers and misunderstandings separately from the twelve visitor
sessions. These reviews do not estimate investor demand or conversion uplift.

## Native device and accessibility review

Use physical iPhone and Android devices; record model, OS/browser, connection,
motion/data preferences and the exact build identity. Emulation is useful
engineering evidence and does not complete this gate.

Check first paint and first motion, an uninterrupted sustained loop, Play/Pause,
offscreen pause, background return, rotation, autoplay refusal, startup failure
and Retry. Confirm one rendition per visit, no audio, stable requested/modeled
quantities, and a finished no-JavaScript or reduced-motion illustration.

Perform a manual keyboard and screen-reader walkthrough of the homepage and
guided rack service sequence. Review focus restoration below the sticky header,
logical reading order, announcement restraint, zoom/reflow and control names.
Inspect the complete encoded loop for flash safety; sparse light design alone is
not an independent flash-safety review.
