## Rendering and editorial contract

- `src/content/research.ts` is the canonical public external-research catalog. It contains stable IDs, metadata, setting, supported sentence, limitations, conflicts, review depth and update-check coverage. Actual review dates remain literal; builds never refresh them automatically.
- `ResearchNotes` renders three HTML entries with exact visible limits and a descriptive link to `/evidence#research`. `ResearchBibliography` renders the seven full references in a distinct `id="research"` section. Both are server components with optional `className`; they add no client island or event handler.
- Existing page owners integrate the sections. Do not place external sources among GridNinja evidence-stage badges or describe peer review as independence from commercial interests. Keep all limitations visible; no hover-only or collapsed disclosure.
- The user is the editorial owner. Any change to a sentence, source, date, limitation or status requires inspection of the supporting source and surrounding copy. A later correction/retraction requires reassessment and a recorded content change, never silent date refresh. Do not alter immutable GridNinja examples to reflect an outside study.
- New research figures or publisher/academic logos require a separately scoped rights and endorsement review; none are included here.
