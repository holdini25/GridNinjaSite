  - region "Proof before autonomy":
    - paragraph: Proof before autonomy
    - heading "Evidence informs a decision. Authority stays explicit." [level=1]
    - paragraph: A modeled result, a useful business outcome, acceptance of a report, and permission to operate are different things. A capacity assessment must keep those boundaries visible.
    - link "Scope an assessment":
      - /url: /assessment?source=proof-hero#scope
    - link "See a sample decision brief":
      - /url: /demo?scenario=b&version=1.0.0&perspective=business#decision-brief
  - heading "What each result means" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Model screening uses allow / repair / reject / no-proof for a stated profile and evidence set. The labels do not authorize equipment changes or establish safe sellable MW.
  - article:
    - heading "Modeled eligibility" [level=3]
    - paragraph: A workload fits the authored model conditions, requires a revision, fails a stated requirement, or cannot be assessed. A result applies only to its scope and window.
  - article:
    - heading "Commercial usefulness" [level=3]
    - paragraph: The decision owner determines whether a profile meets the service and business need. A smaller modeled increment is not automatically a useful offer.
  - article:
    - heading "Report acceptance" [level=3]
    - paragraph: The customer reviews contracted deliverables against agreed criteria. Accepting a report is not acceptance of an operational action.
  - article:
    - heading "Operating permission" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Facilities and operational authorities control site changes. Engineering, safety, contractual, and regulatory review are separate from this public demonstration.
  - heading "A conditional path to more authority" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Shadow Mode would observe without control. Advisory Mode would put proposals before operators. Bounded Autonomy would require a verified dispatch envelope, explicit permissions, rollback, and audit evidence. Expanded Autonomy would require further evidence. These are development stages, not services proven by the synthetic sample.
  - heading "What evidence must travel with a number" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A capacity report needs the requested and revised profiles, reference load, meter boundary, time interval, binding conditions, assumptions, unknowns, dataset/model versions, and review status. A safety report requires its own supported scope; a software check cannot stand in for it.

  - heading "Community benefits require separate evidence" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A modeled facility result does not establish a community benefit. Claims about local electricity affordability, water use, or effects on shared grid infrastructure require their own scope, evidence, and review. The synthetic sample does not assess these outcomes.
