# Assessment comprehension check

Status: prepared, **not conducted**. No participants, responses or passing result
are claimed. This six-person check is specific to the assessment upgrade; it does
not replace the broader [Civic participant protocol](premium-civic-implementation/participant-validation-protocol.md).

## Recruitment and setup

The research owner recruits six people with relevant experience evaluating AI
cloud, colocation, critical facilities or infrastructure capacity decisions. Aim
for a mix of commercial decision makers and technical evaluators, including people
who have not seen earlier GridNinja drafts. Record each person's relevant role and
prior exposure privately. Employees, authors and implementers cannot substitute
for independent buyer/evaluator comprehension.

Use the exact frozen candidate and record its source hash, build ID and settings.
Choose an ordinary desktop or phone viewport matching the participant's intended
use. Confirm that media and verification behavior match the build under review.
The facilitator must not explain the offer before the timed task. Do not submit
an inquiry, connect operational systems, request facility files or collect secrets.

Obtain permission for notes or recordings. Store identity, consent and raw notes
in an authorized private research location, outside this repository. Public release
evidence may contain aggregate results and anonymized participant references only.

## Sixty-second task

Start at Home with an empty history and read this neutral prompt:

> Imagine your team is considering its next capacity commitment. Explore this
> website and explain what you could engage GridNinja to do, what your team would
> need to provide, what you would receive, when paid work begins, and what you
> would do next. You have 60 seconds. Please describe your understanding aloud.

Start the timer when the page is available for inspection. Allow ordinary
navigation and scrolling. Record routes and actions without coaching, pointing
to a CTA, opening disclosures for the participant, or defining terms. Stop the
timed task at 60 seconds. Preserve incomplete or incorrect answers as observed.

## Scoring rubric

Score the participant's own explanation against all five facts. Exact marketing
wording is unnecessary; correct meaning is required. Do not infer an answer from
a click or from a sentence the facilitator supplied.

| Fact | Correct meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Offer | A paid, bounded assessment of one capacity decision using authorized historical inputs; not a currently shipped live control service. |
| Inputs | A decision and owner, facility boundary, proposed commitment or workload, and permission/readiness for the relevant historical inputs. |
| Deliverables | A decision brief and supporting model record, with assumptions, evidence gaps and unresolved questions for review. |
| Paid-work boundary | Scoping first reviews fit/readiness; price, schedule, deliverables and review rounds are agreed before paid work starts. Opening or submitting an inquiry does not purchase an assessment. |
| Next step | Scope an assessment through an inquiry, with the sample decision brief available for inspection. No operational files or credentials should be submitted through the website. |

A participant passes only when all five facts are correctly explained within the
60-second task. Record any claim of operating authority, guaranteed capacity,
guaranteed economics, published pricing, or a fixed turnaround as a misconception,
even if the remaining facts are correct. A useful negative or inconclusive result
is an optional follow-up probe after timing, not a coached correction to the score.

The formative gate passes when at least **five of six relevant evaluators** pass.
Report the numerator and denominator plus fact-level failures. Missing sessions,
missing notes and unknown answers cannot count as passes. Do not extrapolate this
small formative study to a conversion rate or population-level performance claim.

## Private result record and decision

For each session retain: anonymized participant reference, role/relevance, prior
exposure, consent reference, facilitator, date, device/viewport, frozen candidate
identity, task duration, routes visited, verbatim observations, five fact scores,
misconceptions and overall pass/fail. Keep the initial result if a later retest is
conducted; record retests separately with the changed candidate and prior exposure.

An independent reviewer checks scoring against the notes. If fewer than five pass,
document the recurring misunderstanding, make a scoped correction, and requalify
the changed candidate. Do not change the rubric or recruit replacement participants
solely to remove failed observations. The release record names the research owner
and reviewer, links private evidence by access-controlled reference, and reports
the aggregate outcome without exposing participants or commercial information.
