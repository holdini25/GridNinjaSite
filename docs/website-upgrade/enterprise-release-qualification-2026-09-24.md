Their logs and process samples are preserved; the user's open Blender GUI and global preferences were left intact. Final website posters and service-view captures independently verify the actual Apple M5 Pro Metal browser renderer. A CPU bake is not reported as a successful Metal bake.

The [CPU worker calibration](../../build/qa/enterprise-rc21/compute/results.md) ran the identical 36-job GLB hash/metadata/Khronos/Brotli batch three times at each of 2, 4 and 6 workers. Median wall times were **516.8 / 327.8 / 272.4 ms**; six workers were 16.9% faster than four for this short, CPU-only workload. Peak sampled coordinator-plus-worker RSS was 817.5 MiB, with no observed new swapping. Coordinator event-loop delay was a CPU proxy, not browser responsiveness or INP. These 0.27–0.53-second batches do not establish sustained behavior or optimal build, test, Blender or mixed CPU/GPU concurrency. The separately reported child-only peak-RSS sum is not an upper bound for the aggregate. Raw evidence is preserved, with later reporting and harness-limitations notes linked from the results.

Build workers are capped per process; source/master writes and asset registration are serialized. Final browser performance gets exclusive machine time. CPU/GPU unified memory is counted as one shared budget. Simulator evidence remains distinct from physical-phone GPU, memory-pressure, thermal and energy qualification.
